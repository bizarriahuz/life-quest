Preview

One app for the life you're building.

Life Quest brings your prayers, habits, goals, training, sleep, reading, money, friendships and hobbies into one calm app, then turns your progress into something you can see.

Every prayer prayed, run logged, page read, friend called and budget kept earns XP toward nine stats: Strength, Discipline, Knowledge, Reflection, Craft, Faith, Rest, Connection and Wealth. They all add up to one level, your level.

Use only the sections that fit your life, and make it yours with 15 colourways, from pure black-and-white to soft pastels. Everything syncs between your phone and laptop, and your data is locked to your account.
